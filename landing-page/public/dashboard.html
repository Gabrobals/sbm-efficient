🔬 Technical Deep Dive: Why Adaptive-K Works

The MoE Efficiency Problem

Traditional MoE models use fixed top-K routing: every token activates exactly K experts regardless of complexity. DeepSeek-V3 activates 37B of 671B parameters (5.5%), but still wastes compute on trivial tokens that need fewer experts.

Adaptive-K Solution

Token Complexity Fixed K=8 Adaptive-K Savings
Trivial ("the", "a", punctuation) 8 experts 1-2 experts 75-87%
Simple (common words) 8 experts 2-4 experts 50-75%
Moderate (domain terms) 8 experts 4-6 experts 25-50%
Complex (reasoning, code) 8 experts 6-8 experts 0-25%

Key Innovations

Entropy-Based Selection

Router confidence determines K. High entropy (uncertainty) → more experts. Low entropy → fewer experts.

Per-Layer Adaptation

Early layers need fewer experts (pattern matching). Later layers need more (reasoning).

Zero Accuracy Loss

Validated <0.5% accuracy drop across all tested models (Mixtral, OLMoE, Qwen-MoE).
